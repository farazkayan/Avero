Why Avero is under maintenance

Avero is temporarily unavailable while we strengthen how it protects your private memories and personal data. Here's a little more about what's happening, and why.

Why access is paused

We've temporarily paused access so this work can be done thoroughly and safely. While Avero is in maintenance, no one — including signed-in members — can reach the application.

What we're working on

We're strengthening Avero's security and improving how your data is protected, including work on encryption — the practice of securing data so only the people meant to see it can. The goal is simple: your private memories should stay private.

Why we're taking our time

Care matters more than speed here. You've entrusted your memories to us, and we'd rather take the time to complete this work properly than reopen Avero before it's ready.

When Avero will return

Avero will become accessible again once the improvements are complete. We'll share more as the work progresses — we won't reopen in a rush.
