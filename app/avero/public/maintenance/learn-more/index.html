Why Avero is under maintenance

Why Avero is unavailable

We have temporarily paused access to Avero while we complete a major security upgrade. This is a deliberate step to protect what you have entrusted to us.

What we're working on

We are strengthening the security of Avero and improving how your data is encrypted. The goal is simple: your private memories should stay private, and be protected by stronger safeguards than ever.

What this means for your data

Your memories and data remain protected and private. Access is restricted to everyone — including signed-in users — while this work is underway, so that improvements can be made safely.

When Avero will return

Avero will remain unavailable until maintenance is complete. We will restore access as soon as the upgrade has been finished and verified.
