\# Vision



Life moves faster than we realize.



The conversations fade.



The inside jokes are forgotten.



The photos remain.



But the stories behind them slowly disappear.


Avero exists so they don't.



Avero is a private home for the moments people experience together. It helps friends, families, couples, and groups preserve not just what happened, but why it mattered.



Years from now, opening Avero should feel like stepping back into a moment—not scrolling through a gallery.



Our goal is simple:



To make sure the people and experiences that shape our lives are never reduced to forgotten files in a camera roll.



\---



\# Mission



Avero's mission is to give every friendship, family, couple, and group a private place to preserve the moments that shape their story.



We believe memories are more meaningful when they are shared. A photo alone captures what happened, but the people, emotions, conversations, and stories behind it are what make it matter.



Avero helps people collect those moments in one place, organize them over time, and revisit them years later with the same sense of connection they felt when they happened.



Our goal is not to store more media.



Our goal is to help people remember more of their lives.


# Core Philosophy



\## 1. Moments Over Media



Photos and videos are important, but they are not the Memory.



The story, the people, the emotions, and the experience behind them are what truly matter.



Avero exists to preserve moments—not just media.



\---



\## 2. People First



Every Memory begins with people.



Spaces are built around relationships, not individual profiles.



The product should always strengthen shared experiences rather than individual attention.



\---



\## 3. Story Over Chronology



Dates provide context.



Stories provide meaning.



While Avero organizes memories over time, every Memory should feel like a meaningful chapter of a shared story rather than just another item in a timeline.



\---



\## 4. Private by Default



Avero is a personal place.



Memories belong to the people who created them.



Privacy should always be the default, never an afterthought.



Users should always understand who can access their memories.



\---



\## 5. Simplicity is Premium



The best interface is often the one that removes unnecessary choices.



Every screen should feel calm, focused, and intentional.



If something can be simplified without reducing its value, it should be.



\---



\## 6. Built for Longevity



A Memory added today should still feel meaningful years from now.



Avoid trends that may quickly become outdated.



Design the product to age gracefully.



\---



\## 7. Reliability Builds Trust



Every Memory is valuable.



Losing a Memory is far worse than delaying a feature.



Reliability, stability, and data integrity always take priority over shipping quickly.



\---



\## 8. Every Feature Earns Its Place



New features should never exist simply because other apps include them.



Every addition must clearly support Avero's mission of preserving meaningful shared memories.



If it does not strengthen the experience, it does not belong.



\---



\## 9. Consistency Creates Comfort



Consistency is part of the product experience.



Language, spacing, animations, interactions, colors, and components should always feel unified.



Users should never have to relearn how Avero works.



\---



\## 10. Build for the Memory, Not the Moment



Short-term excitement should never outweigh long-term value.



Every decision should answer one question:



\*\*"Will this help someone smile when they open Avero years from now?"\*\*

\# Core Concepts



\## User



A User is an individual account within Avero.



Users can create Spaces, join Spaces through invitations, contribute memories, and manage their own account settings.



A User owns their account, but memories inside a Space are shared with the members of that Space.



\---



\## Space



A Space is the heart of Avero.



It is a private, shared home where people preserve memories together.



Every Space represents a relationship or group, such as close friends, family, a couple, teammates, or classmates.



Each Space has its own members, memories, media, timeline, and statistics.



\---



\## Memory



A Memory is the primary object inside a Space.



A Memory represents a meaningful shared experience rather than a single photo or video.



It can include a title, description, date, media, location, reactions, comments, tags, and other details that help tell the complete story.



Every Memory should answer one simple question:



\*\*"What happened, and why was it meaningful?"\*\*



\---



\## Media



Media includes the photos and videos attached to a Memory.



Media exists to support the story, not replace it.



Every piece of media always belongs to a specific Memory.



Media cannot exist independently inside a Space.



\---



\## Tag



Tags are lightweight labels that help organize Memories.



Examples include:



\* Football

\* Food

\* Travel

\* School

\* All-Nighter



Tags improve discovery without changing the story itself.



\---



\## Location



A Location represents where a Memory happened.



Locations are optional.



They allow Memories to appear on maps, timelines, and future location-based features while adding meaningful context to the story.



\---



\## Reaction



Reactions are quick emotional responses from members of a Space.



They provide a simple way to acknowledge and relive a Memory without needing to write a comment.



Reactions should feel personal and lightweight.



\---



\## Comment



Comments allow members of a Space to add additional context, stories, inside jokes, or perspectives to a Memory.



Different people often remember the same event differently.



Comments help preserve those unique perspectives and make shared memories richer over time.

# Product Principles



\## 1. Capture Should Be Effortless



Adding a Memory should feel quick and natural.



The process should never interrupt the moment itself.



Only ask for information that provides lasting value.



\---



\## 2. Every Screen Has a Purpose



Every screen should solve one clear problem.



Avoid clutter, unnecessary actions, or features that distract from the primary goal.



\---



\## 3. Beautiful by Default



Avero should feel premium without requiring customization.



Thoughtful spacing, typography, animations, and colors should create a calm, polished experience from the very first launch.



\---



\## 4. Consistency Builds Confidence



The same actions should always behave the same way.



Buttons, navigation, icons, spacing, and interactions should remain predictable throughout the product.



\---



\## 5. Feedback for Every Action



Users should never wonder if something happened.



Every important action should provide clear visual feedback through animations, messages, or state changes.



\---



\## 6. Empty States Should Inspire



An empty Space is not an error.



It is the beginning of a story.



Every empty state should encourage users to create their next Memory rather than simply stating that nothing exists.



\---



\## 7. Performance Is Part of the Experience



Fast interactions create trust.



The interface should feel responsive, smooth, and lightweight, even as a Space grows over time.



\---



\## 8. Mobile and Desktop Are Equal



Avero should feel natural on every supported device.



No platform should feel like an afterthought.



\---



\## 9. Reduce Friction



Every additional click, tap, or required field should have a clear purpose.



Whenever possible, simplify without reducing meaning.



\---



\## 10. Reliability Over Speed



It is better to delay a feature than to risk losing someone's memories.



Protecting user data and preserving trust will always take priority over shipping faster.

# Target Users



Avero is built for people who want to preserve meaningful shared experiences with the people who matter most.



It is designed for small, trusted groups where memories have lasting emotional value rather than public attention.



Avero is ideal for:



\* Close friends

\* Families

\* Couples

\* Sports teams

\* Classmates

\* Travel groups

\* Clubs and communities with strong personal connections



These groups create stories together over months and years. Avero gives them a private place to preserve those stories in one shared home.



\---



\## Who Avero Is Not Built For



Avero is not a social media platform.



It is not built for influencers, content creators, or public audiences seeking likes, followers, or viral content.



It is not intended to replace messaging applications or cloud storage services.



People looking to broadcast their lives to strangers are not Avero's audience.



Avero is built for people who want to remember life with the people who lived it beside them.



\---



# Product Identity



\## Avero Is



Avero is a private, shared Memory platform built around meaningful relationships.



It provides a dedicated home where people can preserve the experiences they share together through memories, media, conversations, and context.



Rather than simply storing photos and videos, Avero helps people preserve the stories behind them.



Every Space grows into a living collection of shared experiences that becomes more valuable over time.



Avero is designed to be opened years later, allowing people to relive moments with the same people who experienced them.



\---



\## Avero Is Not



Avero is not a social media platform.



It is not designed around followers, public profiles, popularity, algorithms, or engagement metrics.



It is not a messaging application, cloud storage service, or productivity tool.



It does not encourage endless scrolling or content consumption.



Avero exists to preserve meaningful shared memories—not to compete for attention.



Every feature should strengthen relationships and memories rather than maximize screen time.

# MVP Scope



The first version of Avero should focus on one goal:



Allow a small group of people to preserve, organize, and revisit their shared memories in a simple, reliable, and enjoyable way.



The MVP should include only the features required to fulfill this goal.



\## Included



\* User authentication

\* Private Spaces

\* Space invitations

\* Capture, edit, organize, and delete Memories
\* Photo and video uploads

\* Memory timeline

\* Tags

\* Comments

\* Reactions

\* Search

\* Responsive design for desktop and mobile



\## Not Included



The following ideas are intentionally postponed until after the MVP:



\* AI features

\* Maps

\* Memory recaps

\* Echoes

\* Statistics

\* Achievements

\* Calendar view

\* Notifications

\* Public Spaces

\* Public profiles

\* Messaging

\* Any feature that does not directly improve preserving shared memories



The MVP should prioritize reliability, simplicity, and polish over the number of available features.

# Long-Term Vision



Avero is built with the belief that the value of a Memory grows over time.



Years after a moment has passed, the conversations, photos, videos, and stories surrounding it become even more meaningful.



Our long-term vision is to create the best place for preserving shared memories throughout a person's life.



As Avero evolves, new features should deepen the experience of remembering rather than distract from it. Every addition should strengthen the connection between people and the moments they shared.



Future versions may introduce new ways to rediscover memories, visualize shared journeys, and celebrate important moments together, but they should always remain true to Avero's core philosophy.



Success is not measured by time spent in the app.



Success is measured by the number of meaningful moments people choose to preserve—and the joy they feel when revisiting them years later.



Avero should become something people return to throughout their lives, not because they have to, but because every visit reminds them of the people and experiences that shaped who they are.
